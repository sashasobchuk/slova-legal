Privacy Policy for Slova

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Slova (“the extension”, “we”) is a browser extension that shows language-learning subtitles over video and keeps a personal dictionary on your device. This policy explains what data the extension handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The extension is developed by an individual developer (“the developer”).

1. Summary

  • Your dictionary, saved words, settings and study progress stay on your device. We have no server and do not receive them.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are sent to Google Analytics only if you press “Allow” in the consent banner. You can change your mind at any time.
  • We never collect or send the addresses of pages or sites you visit, video titles, subtitle text, the words you look up, or your translations to our analytics.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data for advertising, profiling or credit assessment.

2. Data stored only on your device

The extension stores the following locally in your browser (IndexedDB and chrome.storage). It is not transmitted to the developer:

  • your dictionary: saved words, translations, study status, interaction history and cached translations, and for each saved word the video ID, site and subtitle sentence where you saved it;
  • settings: interface, learning and translation languages, subtitle appearance, enabled sites, time statistics;
  • keys for optional LLM providers that you enter yourself (see section 4).

To detect subtitle files, the extension checks the addresses of network requests in your browser. It downloads and uses subtitle files only on sites where you enabled it. This happens locally and is not sent to the developer.

Uninstalling the extension or clearing its data removes this local data.

3. Anonymous usage statistics (optional, off until you consent)

When you first use the extension, a banner on the page asks whether you want to help improve Slova. Nothing is sent to analytics before you press Allow. If you press Don’t send (or ignore the banner), no analytics data is sent.

If you allow it, the extension sends the following to Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) through the Measurement Protocol:

  • a random anonymous installation ID, generated only after you consent (it is not linked to your name, e-mail or Google account);
  • an approximate location (country) that Google may derive from the IP address of the request; we do not receive or store IP addresses;
  • interface language, the language of the subtitles you watch, and your translation language;
  • extension version and browser name; on an update, also the previous extension version;
  • a session identifier (the start time of the session);
  • the name of the video platform where subtitles were opened, chosen from a fixed list (for example YouTube, Netflix, or “other”), and whether subtitles loaded successfully or the failure reason;
  • usage time and the number of days since installation.

Never sent: page or site addresses, video titles, subtitle text, the words you look up, translations, your dictionary, or any name or e-mail.

Legal basis (for users in the EEA/UK): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time: open the extension’s Options, page “Data and synchronization”, section “Anonymous statistics”, and uncheck the box. Withdrawing deletes the stored installation ID from your device, so later events cannot be linked to earlier ones, and no further data is sent.

This data is received and processed by Google Analytics under Google’s terms: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Third-party services you trigger

  • Translation. When you open a translation for a word or phrase, that text and the language pair are sent to Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com). If you choose another engine in Options (Chrome’s on-device translator or your own translation server URL), the text goes there instead. If the selected engine returns no result, the extension may fall back to another engine, including Google Translate. Results are cached on your device.
  • Example sentences. If you open the Tatoeba tab in the word popup, the word and language pair are sent to Tatoeba (api.tatoeba.org).
  • Local AI models (optional). If you enable local language models, the model files and runtime code are downloaded from Hugging Face, jsDelivr and GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com); the models run on your device. The extension also downloads a static lemma (word base form) file from GitHub; it contains no data about you, but GitHub sees the request like any web server.
  • Your own LLM provider (optional). If you configure an LLM provider in Options (for example OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, a local Ollama/LM Studio server, or a custom URL), the selected word and the surrounding subtitle sentence are sent to that provider or URL under its terms. The API key is stored only on your device.
  • Subtitle files. Subtitle files are requested from the video platform itself (for example YouTube) with your browser session, as your browser would when playing the video.
  • Phone sync (optional). If you pair the extension with the Slova mobile app, dictionary data is exchanged directly between your devices over your local network (unencrypted HTTP on port 9999). No server of ours is involved. Use it only on a network you trust.

5. Uninstall feedback form

When you uninstall the extension, your browser may open an optional feedback form (Google Forms). The link contains the extension version, days since installation and browser name; if you had consented to statistics, it also contains your interface, learning and translation languages. Answering the form is voluntary. The form is hosted by Google Forms: answers are available to the developer and processed by Google under its terms.

6. Sharing, selling and use limits

  • We do not sell or rent data and do not transfer it to third parties, except the services described in sections 3–5.
  • Data is used only to operate and improve the extension. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or credit assessment.
  • The extension’s use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Local data can be managed or deleted by you in the extension. Because analytics uses a random ID that is never linked to your identity, we generally cannot identify your records; turning the statistics off in Options stops all further collection and removes the ID. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Analytics requests are sent over HTTPS. Data on your device is protected by your browser profile. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children

Slova is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. If a change expands analytics collection, we will update this policy and the notice in the extension.

11. Contact
