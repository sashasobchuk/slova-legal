Privacy Policy for the Slova mobile app

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Applies to the Slova app for Android and iOS. The Slova browser extension has its own policy.

Slova (“the app”, “we”) helps you learn words from YouTube and TikTok subtitles, books and a card deck of words. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The app is developed by an individual developer (“the developer”).

1. Summary

  • Your dictionary, saved words, books, settings and study progress stay on your device. We have no server that receives them and no user accounts.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are sent to Google Firebase only if you press “Allow” in the consent dialog. You can change your mind at any time in the “About” screen.
  • We never send the text of words or sentences you look up, video links or titles, book content, your e-mail or any other personal data to analytics.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use the Advertising ID. Google signals and ad personalization are disabled.

2. Data stored only on your device

The app stores the following locally. It is not transmitted to the developer:

  • your dictionary: saved words, translations, study status and history;
  • books you import (files and text), covers, and your reading position;
  • settings: interface, learning and translation languages, translation engine, theme;
  • keys and addresses of optional AI providers that you enter yourself (see section 4); keys are kept in the device’s secure storage.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this local data. The app also lets you make a backup file of your data; where you save or send that file is your choice.

3. Anonymous analytics and crash reports (optional, off until you consent)

When you first open the app, a dialog asks whether you want to help improve Slova. Nothing is sent before you press Allow. If you press Not now, no analytics or crash data is sent.

If you allow it, the app sends the following to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC):

  • a random app-instance ID created by Firebase (not linked to your name, e-mail or Google account);
  • an approximate location (country) that Google may derive from the IP address of the request; we do not receive or store IP addresses;
  • interface language, the language you learn and your translation language, the chosen translation engine, and the region of your device (for example “UA”);
  • app version, operating system and version, device model, and session information collected automatically by Firebase;
  • the names of the app screens you open and the time spent on them;
  • counts and kinds of actions, without their content: for example that a video was opened (from YouTube or TikTok), that subtitles loaded or failed and how long it took, how many words were saved, how many cards you swiped left or right, or that a book was imported;
  • error codes and, for Crashlytics, technical crash and error reports (stack traces, device state at the time of the crash). An error message in such a report may rarely contain a fragment of technical text, but the app does not put the words, sentences, videos or books you work with into reports.

Never sent to analytics: the text of words, sentences or translations, video links, IDs and titles, book content, your dictionary, e-mail, name or contacts. The app does not use the Advertising ID (IDFA / AAID) and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Legal basis (for users in the EEA/UK): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). To withdraw consent, open the “About” screen and turn off “Send anonymous statistics”. Sending stops immediately. Data already sent stays with Google and is deleted under Google’s retention settings (analytics events are kept for a limited period configured in Google Analytics).

This data is received and processed by Google under Google’s terms: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Third-party services you trigger

  • YouTube and TikTok. Videos open inside the app in a web view and load from YouTube or TikTok. These sites process your request under their own terms and may set cookies. Subtitles are requested from the video platform with your browser session in that web view.
  • Translation. When you open a translation for a word or phrase, that text and the language pair may be sent to Google Translate. If you choose the on-device or offline translation engines, the text stays on your device. If the selected engine returns no result, the app may fall back to another engine.
  • Example sentences and dictionaries. If you open the Tatoeba tab or an online dictionary link, the word and language pair are sent to that site (for example Tatoeba) under its terms.
  • Offline translation and language models. If you download offline models, the model files and code are downloaded from Hugging Face, jsDelivr, unpkg and GitHub; the models run on your device. The app also loads UI translations and static data files from GitHub. These servers see the request like any web server.
  • AI chat (optional). If you configure an AI provider yourself (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter or your own server), the messages you write and the context you choose are sent to that provider under its terms. The app has no built-in AI provider; the key is stored only on your device.
  • Voice dictation (optional). If you use dictation in the chat, the app asks for microphone permission. Speech recognition is done by your device’s speech service (Apple or Google). Depending on the language and your device settings it may run on-device or send audio to that provider under its terms.
  • Phone sync (optional). If you pair the app with the Slova browser extension, data is exchanged directly between your devices over your local network. No server of ours is involved. Use it only on a network you trust.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone — only for voice dictation in the chat; you can refuse it.
  • Photos / files — only to choose a book cover or import a book file you pick.
  • Network — to load videos, subtitles, translations and models.

6. Sharing, selling and use limits

  • We do not sell or rent data and do not transfer it to third parties, except the services described in sections 3–4.
  • Data is used only to operate and improve the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling or credit assessment.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Local data can be managed or deleted by you in the app. Because analytics uses a random ID that is never linked to your identity, we generally cannot identify your records; turning statistics off in the “About” screen stops all further collection. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Analytics and crash reports are sent over HTTPS. Data on your device is protected by your device’s security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children

Slova is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. If a change expands data collection, we will update this policy and the consent dialog in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sashasobchuk/slova-legal/issues.
